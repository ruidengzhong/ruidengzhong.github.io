## Course Overview

This course covers computer structure, assembly language, instruction execution, addressing techniques, and digital representation of data, computer system organization, logic design, microprogramming, cache and memory systems, and input/output interfaces. 

I am a UTA for this course. My responsibilities include:

- Communicating regularly with the course instructor and UTA management team; attending required meetings or trainings
- Submitting timecards accurately and on time; and helping maintain a professional, inclusive, and safe learning environment.
