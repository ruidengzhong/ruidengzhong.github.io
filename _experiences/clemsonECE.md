As a lab aide, I developed and optimized 3D-printing workflows for denture models and biological scaffolds. My work focused on improving print quality, evaluating material performance, and organizing published research on filament properties for biomedical fabrication applications.

I investigated how material selection affected the durability of a 3D-printed denture model and used this analysis to identify materials better suited for repeated mechanical use. I also conducted a literature review on polymers and filament characteristics relevant to scaffold fabrication, including material properties, printability, and potential biomedical applications. The information was organized into a structured database to support future material selection and scaffold-design decisions.

### Some of my work included:

- Developing and refining 3D-printing workflows for denture models and biological scaffolds
- Evaluating filament and polymer options for biomedical additive manufacturing
- Comparing material properties related to durability, strength, printability, and scaffold fabrication
- Optimizing material selection to improve the durability of a 3D-printed denture model
- Reviewing scientific literature on filament properties and scaffold manufacturing
- Extracting and organizing material data from research publications
- Developing a searchable database of filament properties and fabrication considerations
- Connecting material characteristics to print quality and intended biomedical applications
- Supporting future design decisions through structured literature-based analysis
- Applying additive-manufacturing concepts to dental and tissue-engineering applications

### Skills Developed: 
- Additive Manufacturing: FDM/filament-based 3D printing, print-process development, workflow optimization
- Biomedical Fabrication: Denture modeling, biological scaffolds, dental and tissue-engineering applications
- Materials Selection: Polymer and filament comparison, durability evaluation, printability analysis
- Literature Research: Scientific literature review, technical data extraction, research synthesis
- Data Organization: Material-property databases, structured data collection, comparative analysis
- Engineering Analysis: Relating material properties to mechanical performance and manufacturing requirements
- Research Communication: Summarizing technical findings and supporting design decisions with published research
