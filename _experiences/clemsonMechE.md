## Work in context

Working with Dr. Hai Xiao, I studied whether controlled compression could produce a structure resembling long-range ordered porous carbon (LOPC). Instead, the simulations revealed a stable deformed configuration in which upper-layer fullerenes shifted into spaces between the lower layers. I presented these findings in a poster session at ASME IMECE 2024 in Portland, Oregon.
