## Teaching in context

I serve as an undergraduate teaching assistant for **ECE/CS 250 — Computer Architecture**, taught by **Prof. Daniel J. Sorin** in Fall 2026. The course explores how computer hardware executes software, connecting low-level programming with the organization of a computer system.

Course topics include computer structure, assembly language, instruction execution, addressing techniques, digital data representation, logic design, microprogramming, cache and memory systems, and input/output interfaces. Through recitations, student support, and feedback, I help students develop their understanding of these concepts.
